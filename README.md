## 👋 About me

Software engineer based in Santiago, Chile. I work across the full stack — APIs, infra, and the
pixel-level UI — and I'm deepening into data science on the side.

## ✨ What I like to do

I like taking a product from idea to something real people use, usually solo: sketch it, build the
backend, ship the UI, wire up the infra. Outside of that I enjoy small, playful engineering — terminal
tools and CLI-first experiences, like a [colorful ASCII car](https://runcar.dev) you get by running
`curl runcar.dev`, or a [pack of terminal games](https://github.com/ngutierrezp/curl-games) written
in portable Bash.
